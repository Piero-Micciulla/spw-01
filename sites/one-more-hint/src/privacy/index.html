PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE MYSTERY

Nothing up our sleeve.

One More Hint does not require an account. It currently uses no analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking technology.

The game has no backend and no runtime AI. Puzzle answers, your choices, and your session score are not sent anywhere. Session state exists only in the page’s memory and normally resets when you refresh or close it.

If you choose Share, your browser may offer its built-in sharing interface. If that is unavailable, the game copies spoiler-free challenge text to your clipboard. Those browser features are used only after you press the Share button.

Future analytics or advertising integration points are disabled. If the product changes, this page should change before those features are enabled.
