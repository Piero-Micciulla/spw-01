THE SHORT VERSION

Your list
stays here.

Party Buy List has no account, backend or database. The party configuration, checkmarks and custom items are processed on your device.

Local storage

Checklist progress and custom items are saved in your browser’s localStorage, separately for each party configuration. Reset removes checkmarks for the current plan; browser settings can remove all local data.

Sharing

Shared links contain only the small party configuration needed to recreate recommendations. They never include checkmarks or custom items.

No tracking

There are no analytics, ads, affiliate links, cookies, retailer APIs or enabled external services.
