    What you’re looking at

    Every outline uses the source length, overall body width, height, and wheelbase in millimetres. Top view is centred lengthwise and laterally. Side view places wheel centres at the source wheelbase, with the remaining length split evenly only to locate the generic silhouette. The shape is intentionally abstract; the geometry is the point.

    “Bounding footprint” means length × body width, not tyre contact area, cabin room, turning circle, or garage clearance.
