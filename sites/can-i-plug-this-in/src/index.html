    KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

    An adapter is not a converter.

    PLUG

    Physical fit

    A plug adapter changes the connection shape. It does not change voltage or frequency.

    VOLTAGE

    Electrical pressure

    Your device’s complete input range must cover the local nominal supply. A mismatch can damage equipment or create a hazard.

    FREQUENCY

    Alternating current rate

    50 Hz and 60 Hz are not interchangeable for every device. A transformer does not normally change frequency.

    What does 100–240V · 50/60Hz mean?

    It means the manufacturer rated that device or power supply for inputs from 100 through 240 volts and for both common mains frequencies. You may still need a plug adapter.

    Voltage-conversion equipment

    If voltage does not match, an appropriate converter or transformer would have to supply the voltage your device requires and handle its actual power. Heating appliances, motors, timing-sensitive and electronically controlled equipment need particular care. This checker does not recommend converter capacity.

    SOURCES / METHODOLOGY

    Conservative by design.

    Country supply records are compiled from the World Standards country table, which references IEC World Plugs conventions. Plug/socket systems standardized by national standards are catalogued in IEC/TR 60083. Adapter and dual-voltage explanations are cross-checked against Electrical Safety First guidance.

    For destinations with multiple published voltages or frequencies, a safe verdict requires the device to cover every listed value. Socket letters are compared exactly; listed socket types are not assumed to accept one another.
