YOUR LIST
STAYS YOURS.

Stock-Up Checklist needs no account. Your selected scenario, checked items, and custom items are stored only in your browser using localStorage so they are waiting when you return.

What leaves your device?

No checklist contents are sent to a server. We do not ask for your location and do not connect to an external shopping API.

Tracking and advertising

There is currently no analytics, advertising, or affiliate tracking. We do not set cookies. The site contains no enabled ad placements or shopping links.

Sharing

Copy and Share use your browser or device. A shared URL identifies only the scenario; it does not contain checked items or custom text.

Clear your data

Reset clears the current scenario. You can clear all saved data by removing this site's local storage in your browser settings.
