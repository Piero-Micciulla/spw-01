LOCAL DATA ONLY

Your office plan stays in your browser.

01

No account or backend

The planner runs entirely in your browser. Your office choices and checklist are not sent to us.

02

Local storage

Checked items are saved in localStorage under an identity derived from the plan configuration. A different configuration gets a separate checklist.

03

Sharing

Shared links contain only validated planner choices: headcount, style, computer scope, meeting rooms, spaces and tier. Checklist state is never included.

04

No tracking

There are no analytics, ads, affiliate links or cookies. Clear saved checklists through your browser's site-data controls or the plan's reset action.
