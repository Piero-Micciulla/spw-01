THE SHORT VERSION

YOUR TRIP URGE STAYS IN YOUR BROWSER.

Send Me Somewhere has no accounts, backend, database, analytics, advertising, cookies or location access. Your answers are used in memory on your device to choose a destination. Refreshing may reset them.

Sharing

The share button uses your browser’s native sharing feature where available, with a clipboard fallback. A shared URL contains only a validated destination identifier, such as ?to=porto. It does not contain your preferences.

External services

The site makes no runtime requests to travel, weather, AI, advertising or affiliate services. Destination sources are provenance metadata used during curation and are not loaded by the page.

Contact

This local production build does not publish a contact address. Add an operator contact before public deployment if required by your jurisdiction.
