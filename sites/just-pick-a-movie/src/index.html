How does the picker work?

It chooses from a hand-curated local catalog, using only the time and mood you select. No account, algorithmic profile, or movie API is involved.

02

I only have 90 minutes.

Choose “95 min or less.” We use a little breathing room for credits and runtime variations, while keeping the pick genuinely short.

03

What do the moods mean?

Moods are broad editorial tags, not scientific categories. A movie can be funny and scary, or chill and serious. Pick the feeling, not a database genre.

04

Why give me only one?

Because another ranked list is still a decision. This tool is for the moment when choosing has become less fun than watching.

05

What if I hate the pick?

Reject it. “Pick another” avoids immediate repeats and works through unseen eligible movies before cycling back.

06

Where can I stream it?

Availability changes by country and week, so we don’t guess. Search your own services once the decision is made.
