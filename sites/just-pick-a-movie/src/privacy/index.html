The fine print

Privacy,
plainly.

Just Pick a Movie does not currently use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts, cookies, or third-party movie services.

The picker keeps a short history of recommendations in your browser’s session storage so “Pick another” can avoid immediate repeats. This history stays on your device, contains only movie IDs, and is normally cleared when the browser session ends.

A chosen movie may appear in the page address so you can share it. It is validated against the local catalog. The site does not send the choice to us. A future hosting provider may retain standard server logs for security and reliability.

If analytics or advertising is enabled later, this page and any required consent controls will be updated before tracking begins.
