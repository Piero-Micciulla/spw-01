Your draft never leaves the desk.

The repeated-word and phrase analysis happens entirely inside your browser. Your pasted text is not sent to a server, an AI service, an API, or any other third party.

Nothing saved

There is no account. Draft text is not placed in localStorage, cookies, URLs, or query parameters. Closing or refreshing this page clears it.

Nothing watching

There are currently no analytics, advertisements, affiliate links, or tracking pixels. The site makes no runtime network request for your writing.

Copying results

The optional copy action copies only the analysis summary—never the source draft. Copying is initiated by you and uses your browser clipboard.
